Youth Golf Program

Photo needed. The previous image on this page was a GoDaddy stock photo and was not reused. A photo of the junior program can go here.

At Go Golf, our junior programs are built around one thing: helping kids improve their golf game in a fun, structured, and encouraging environment. Our junior group classes and camps are designed for players of all skill levels, from beginners learning the basics to more advanced juniors looking to sharpen their skills and lower their scores.

Our junior golf clinics focus on the fundamentals every young golfer needs, including proper grip, stance, posture, swing mechanics, chipping and putting. Juniors also learn valuable on-course skills like golf etiquette, rules, and course strategy, helping them feel confident both on the range and on the course.

During our junior golf camps, kids get the opportunity to practice all areas of the game while working alongside other junior golfers. Camps include instruction, skill-building games, and competitive challenges that keep learning exciting while still emphasizing real improvement. Group setting also help juniors stay motivated, build friendships in the sport, and learn how to perform under pressure.

Our goal at Go Golf is to provide a high-quality junior experience that helps kids build strong fundamentals, develop confidence, and create a lifelong love for the game.
