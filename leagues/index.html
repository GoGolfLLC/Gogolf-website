Format published on the current site

Week 1&2 will play with no handicap. After week 2 using a special algorithm a hcp will be generated for each player.

There will be a Gross & a Net winner for the 10 weeks. 1 Player cannot win both!

FedEx Cup style point system (for Gross & Net)

9 - holes per week; auto-putt; no lie penalty; no mulligans. This is an honor system league!

Play at your own convenience - you get an hour per week of sim time for warm-up and play. IF you want to play on a specific night each week, we will reserve the simulators for you!

IF you know you cannot come on a specific week, you can pre-golf the previous week

20% of the total entry fees will be the prize pool. Winners receive a picture with the Go Golf Cup; their named etched in Go Golf history (of course); Go Golf Shop Credit (can be used for anything excluding lesson programs).

Weeks 1-9 are the Regular Season. Players will be awarded points for where they finish in the weekly rankings. At the conclusion of week 9, players will be reseeded with points respective to their overall position. Week 10 is the Go Golf Cup Championship. These points will be slightly higher giving the lower seeded players an opportunity to still win the overall Tour Championship Title!

Failure to play a round for the week will result in 0 points for the week.
