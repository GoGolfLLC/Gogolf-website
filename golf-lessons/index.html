Golf Evaluation (Click Book Now for Pricing)

The Evaluation begins with a discussion about... you! Have you had golf lessons before? Are you left or right handed? How long have you been playing? Where do you play? How often do you play and/or practice? What's a range of scores? Have you played any sports in your life? What were they? Do you currently/previously have any injuries? Does anything hurt or bother you during or after a round of golf? Evaluations are mandatory for any new client or anyone who hasn't had a lesson in 2 years. Just think.. If your arm hurt, you wouldn't just ask a surgeon to go in and repair it. It might just be bruised. The evaluation will pin point where the focus needs to be and we can come up with a cure!

We then will dive into the putting. I will have you putt a relatively straight put from 8 feet. Video will be taken: Face on, Down the line, and from the ground (Path/face angle).

Following the putting evaluation, we will enter the swing studio. All swings will be recorded using highspeed cameras on the V1 Pro Sports software. All videos will be sent to an e-mail of your choosing. Our simulators are equipped with Foresight GC3 launch monitors and Foresight FSX Play Software.

Three areas of the game will be video, analyzed, and given a game plan to correct the flaws. We will look at chipping, a 50 yard half swing wedge shot, and a 7 iron full swing.

Based on your goals and the timeline to achieve them, we will choose a program that most suits your needs.
